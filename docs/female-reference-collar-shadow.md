# Selected hair, head/collar balance and upper-lid shadow

Style 7 previously used a stripped legacy cutout whose forehead pieces did not match its picker image, and its text called for an open forehead. Current bounded female generation uses a hair-only guide extracted from the actual 512px picker preview, retaining its fine forehead pieces and complete shoulder-side ends. The donor face, eyes, brows and body are excluded. Native kept RGB values match the picker image; hidden transparent RGB is white. Other reference files are unchanged. A female-only hair policy suffix prevents old restored/cached heads from masquerading as the repaired result. Local makeup cannot stamp that new policy onto old hair. Male policy/cache keys remain unchanged.

Female initial composition now uses measured shoulder width for uniform head scaling and a measured inner neckline for chin placement. The narrower central measurement avoids anchoring on high lapel/stole tips. Chin clearance is proportional to face height; crown clearance constrains scale. Both axes retain one scale, and garment pixels/frame stay fixed. Studio suit fitting uses the same neckline/shoulder rule. A new generated hair master on an old unmarked female placement is normalized once; local makeup keeps its existing placement. Manual frames with very high collars can require a smaller head to retain crown clearance.

V6 makeup replaces the central radial eyeshadow spot with a tapered feathered band following the whole upper lid. Previous forehead-light blending, liner/lashes, native dimensions, alpha and eye-interior protection remain. The old saved image is not repainted without an explicit selection; select the hairstyle again to regenerate with the repaired guide/placement, and use Natural → Makeup for the new local palette.

Checks cover the actual style-7 guide and forehead strands, donor-feature/body exclusion, source RGB preservation, female collar geometry, old-layout migration, restored-result cache isolation, 282 geometry plans over all 47 garment PNGs, unchanged garment pixels/frame/pose, Natural restoration, eyeshadow coverage and protected eye pixels at 384/900px. Browser replay of retained real AI cutouts composes both Natural/Makeup with a high-collar gown and an open-collar suit. This checks the client pipeline; exact future AI hair rendering is not guaranteed by deterministic tests.
