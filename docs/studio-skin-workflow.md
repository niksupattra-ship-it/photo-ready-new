# Prepared studio skin

New female portraits use `studio-skin-stages-v3`, for both Natural and the supported makeup choices. This revision targets acne and wrinkle removal, uniform fine skin texture and reduced oily highlights. Existing male requests and unversioned requests retain their previous pipeline. Previously queued v1 and v2 requests retain their original pixels and prompts.

1. Measure face landmarks and semantic skin/hair labels from the exact canonical input. Pack labels to at most 256 pixels on the long edge; MediaPipe can return native-size category masks.
2. Bind the job to the owned work's immutable input. If a saved project or another encoder supplies different canonical bytes, refund the pending reservation and return the frozen input privately. The browser measures that image and retries once within the same submission. The untouched upload remains `originalSource`.
3. Create a separate native-size PNG with bounded shadow fill and broad tone corrections. Estimate global colour casts only when independent near-neutral clothing and background samples agree; preserve natural undertones otherwise. After the native soft-focus pass, v3 repairs localized red acne and wrinkle creases to surrounding healthy skin, harmonizes fine skin microtexture and compresses bright skin hotspots. Protect eyes, brows, lips, nostrils, facial boundaries, identifying moles, alpha and non-skin pixels. Preserve broad anatomical shading and natural complexion rather than darkening the whole face or covering it with a white veil.
4. Send that completed skin PNG, and an identity crop from the same PNG when requested, to the existing single portrait request. The v3 AI policy explicitly removes remaining active pimples, temporary acne marks and visible wrinkles, matches repaired areas to fine uniformly textured adjacent skin, and reduces oily forehead/nose/cheek highlights. Its job includes selective defect repair, rather than merely retaining the source's acne and creases. Preserve identity, facial structure and sharp features; defer added cosmetics.
5. After the returned portrait is levelled, Natural finishes directly. Makeup uses detected output landmarks to add restrained lip colour, upper-lid colour and lashes locally. This final stage does not repeat skin brightening, smoothing, contouring or under-eye colouring.

Processed heads carry separate skin and cosmetic completion versions. Old saved pixels remain visible, but cannot seed the new female caches. Explicitly selecting the existing finish can refresh an old result. Never stamp these completion markers on the upload. Existing job confirmation, cancellation, purchase and export transactions remain in place; there is no new automatic job-resume feature.

Validation includes numerical native-pixel acne, wrinkle, highlight and microtexture checks; actual multipart/job/SQL handlers with inert services; actual local cosmetic rendering; real MediaPipe evidence on existing public application portraits; and clearly labelled synthetic defect fixtures. These checks do not establish that a provider-generated portrait matches an individual customer's preferred skin style. Added fine microtexture supplies a controlled photographic finish, rather than recovering genuine pores lost in a blurred or clipped original. Identifying marks and facial anatomy remain protected.
