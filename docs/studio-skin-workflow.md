# Prepared studio skin

New female portraits use `studio-skin-stages-v4`, for both Natural and the supported makeup choices. This revision adds an identity-free skin/light style guide and adaptive frontal studio relighting to the existing acne/wrinkle repair, fine texture and matte highlights. Existing male and unversioned requests retain their previous pipeline; queued v1, v2 and v3 requests retain their original pixels, prompts and provider image lists.

1. Measure face landmarks and semantic skin/hair labels from the exact canonical input. Pack labels to at most 256 pixels on the long edge; MediaPipe can return native-size category masks.
2. Bind the job to the owned work's immutable input. If a saved project or another encoder supplies different canonical bytes, refund the pending reservation and return the frozen input privately. The browser measures that image and retries once within the same submission. The untouched upload remains `originalSource`.
3. For v4, reject only clearly insufficient native face resolution or catastrophic loss of face/detail exposure before provider generation. Keep usable dark, warm and side-lit photographs eligible. Create a separate native-size PNG with bounded shadow fill and broad tone corrections. Estimate global colour casts only when independent near-neutral clothing and background samples agree; preserve natural undertones otherwise. After the native soft-focus pass, v3 repairs localized red acne and wrinkle creases to surrounding healthy skin, harmonizes fine skin microtexture and compresses bright skin hotspots. Protect eyes, brows, lips, nostrils, facial boundaries, identifying moles, alpha and non-skin pixels. Preserve broad anatomical shading and natural complexion rather than darkening the whole face or covering it with a white veil.
4. Send the completed skin PNG, its same-person identity crop when requested, and the fixed skin/light guide to the existing single portrait request. The v4 AI policy removes remaining active acne and wrinkles and adaptively relights the face, ears and neck toward the guide's broad soft frontal key/fill, smooth skin tone and soft satin highlights. Preserve the person's identity and underlying complexion while correcting ambient casts and excessive directional shadows. The identity crop supplies geometry and identifying detail, not a lock on source exposure or shadow. The final guide supplies finish/light only; never copy a reference person, complexion, makeup, grid or texture pattern. Defer added cosmetics.
5. After the returned portrait is levelled, Natural finishes directly. Makeup uses detected output landmarks to add restrained lip colour, upper-lid colour and lashes locally. This final stage does not repeat skin brightening, smoothing, contouring or under-eye colouring.

Processed heads carry separate skin and cosmetic completion versions. Old saved pixels remain visible, but cannot seed the new female caches. Explicitly selecting the existing finish can refresh an old result. Never stamp these completion markers on the upload. Existing job confirmation, cancellation, purchase and export transactions remain in place; there is no new automatic job-resume feature.

Validation includes numerical native-pixel acne, wrinkle, highlight and microtexture checks; actual multipart/job/SQL handlers with inert services; actual local cosmetic rendering; real MediaPipe evidence on existing public application portraits; and clearly labelled synthetic defect fixtures. These checks do not establish that a provider-generated portrait matches an individual customer's preferred skin style. Added fine microtexture supplies a controlled photographic finish, rather than recovering genuine pores lost in a blurred or clipped original. Identifying marks and facial anatomy remain protected.
