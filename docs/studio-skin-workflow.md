# Prepared studio skin

Current new female portraits use the original-upload AI route described in [original-portrait-ai.md](original-portrait-ai.md). The staged workflow below is retained for older pending requests only.

New female portraits use `studio-skin-stages-v5`, for both Natural and the supported makeup choices. This revision calibrates usable source illumination before skin treatment and repairs discontinuities around protected facial features. Natural and Makeup share a completed skin/light base; the previous local cosmetic palette is added afterward. The identity-free visual guide, acne/wrinkle repair, fine texture and restrained highlights remain. Male and unversioned requests retain their previous pipeline; queued v1–v4 requests retain their original pixels, prompts and provider image lists.

1. Measure face landmarks and semantic skin/hair labels from the exact canonical input. Pack labels to at most 256 pixels on the long edge; MediaPipe can return native-size category masks.
2. Bind the job to the owned work's immutable input. If a saved project or another encoder supplies different canonical bytes, refund the pending reservation and return the frozen input privately. The browser measures that image and retries once within the same submission. The untouched upload remains `originalSource`.
3. Reject only clearly insufficient native face resolution or catastrophic loss of face/detail exposure before provider generation. Keep usable dark, warm and side-lit photographs eligible. In v5, use bilateral, mutually consistent sclera samples as an illumination cue. Correct significant casts or global underexposure with bounded channel gains and highlight roll-off across the whole visible image. A brighter reliable eye supplies the exposure cue when one side is shadowed; uncertain eye samples leave this stage unchanged. This is a photometric correction, not a fixed target skin colour. Geometry, alpha and the owned source remain unchanged.
4. Create a separate native-size surface with the existing broad tone corrections, soft focus, localized acne/wrinkle repair, fine texture and highlight compression. Earlier stages protect expanded eye/brow/lip regions. Those hard exclusions can leave untreated skin near inner brow heads and mouth corners next to treated skin. The v5 continuity pass transfers a smooth neighbouring correction to the surrounding skin while protecting actual feature pigment and edges. Preserve facial boundaries, identifying moles, alpha and broad anatomical shading.
5. Send the completed skin PNG, its same-person identity crop and the fixed skin/light guide to the existing single portrait request. In v5, derive the identity crop from measured evidence when no explicit crop is supplied. The AI removes remaining active acne and wrinkles, keeps skin transitions continuous around facial features, and relights face, ears and neck toward broad soft frontal key/fill and soft satin highlights. Preserve identity and underlying complexion after correcting ambient casts and excessive shadows. The identity crop supplies geometry and identifying detail, not a lock on source exposure. The final guide supplies finish/light only; never copy a reference person, complexion, makeup, grid or texture pattern. All finish choices request the same bare skin policy.
6. After levelling and clothing cleanup, retain the completed bare skin base on the processed portrait. Derive Natural and Makeup from that same base. Makeup uses detected output landmarks to add the previous restrained lip colour, upper-lid colour and lashes locally; it does not repeat skin brightening, smoothing, contouring or under-eye colouring. Eligible finish switches reuse this base and retain the existing job and rights. Keep the base with saved work so a restored portrait can switch finishes consistently. A structurally edited or older head follows the existing regeneration path instead of silently discarding edits.

Processed heads carry separate skin and cosmetic completion versions. Old saved pixels remain visible, but cannot seed the new female caches. Explicitly selecting the existing finish can refresh an old result. Never stamp these completion markers on the upload. Existing job confirmation, cancellation, purchase and export transactions remain in place; there is no new automatic job-resume feature.

Validation includes numerical native-pixel acne, wrinkle, highlight and microtexture checks; actual multipart/job/SQL handlers with inert services; actual local cosmetic rendering; real MediaPipe evidence on existing public application portraits; and clearly labelled synthetic defect fixtures. These checks do not establish that a provider-generated portrait matches an individual customer's preferred skin style. Added fine microtexture supplies a controlled photographic finish, rather than recovering genuine pores lost in a blurred or clipped original. Identifying marks and facial anatomy remain protected.
