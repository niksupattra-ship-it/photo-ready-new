# Continuous studio skin

New female portraits use `studio-skin-stages-v6`. The previous regional base, soft-focus, retouch and four-point continuity passes remain frozen for queued v1–v5 requests. They are not applied to v6 inputs. Their separate masks, feature guards, hard sample skips and rasterized boundaries could give different parts of a face different corrections; repairing four fixed locations did not address that general failure.

1. Measure the exact canonical owned source. Keep immutable original bytes, orientation, source binding, account and job confirmation transactions intact.
2. Check usable face resolution and catastrophic clipping. Normalize reliable bilateral eye-white illumination cues across the whole image. A bounded smooth fill field may lift a strongly shadowed side when both eyes agree about the illuminant. No skin stencil, localized defect patch, face-oval retouch or tile is placed on the input.
3. Use the existing single portrait generation with the same-person identity crop and fixed identity-free light/texture guide. All finish selections request the same bare skin. The AI performs acne/wrinkle cleanup and creates continuous skin colour and diffuse frontal lighting, with a stronger matte-highlight instruction.
4. Level the generated native cutout and clean residual clothing as before. Re-measure output landmarks and semantics. Apply one continuous low-frequency colour/light field over face, ears and retained neck, and compress excessive skin highlights. Measure correction fields at a bounded analysis resolution and bilinearly apply them to native RGB; never resize skin samples or move coordinates. Semantic holes inside plausible facial skin are measurement gaps, not untreated rectangles. Protect actual brow/lip pigment, eye interiors, identifying dark detail, alpha and source bytes. Added grain or artificial pores are not part of this pass.
5. Save the finished native PNG as the shared bare base. Natural returns this base. Makeup applies the existing colour values, restoring the Korean preset's upper-lid colour and upper liner and making its lip tint visible; it adds no second skin-brightening, nose highlight or under-eye-lightening pass. Legacy local-renderer defaults remain unchanged.
6. Eligible local finish switches retain the same accepted job, rights, hair, geometry and base. Undo/redo and saved restore keep parent master/cache and the visible finish consistent. Count the extra base image in existing project limits. Existing v5 bare bases may receive the common surface finish once and switch cosmetics locally while retaining their v5 job/version. Keep them out of v6 generation caches; preserve both old/new base metadata through undo. Structurally edited heads follow their established processing path.

Validation must include the actual application after replacing a source and switching finishes, not only function-level mocks. Compare multiple people and lighting conditions, skin colour islands at changing positions, highlight distributions and native alpha/feature/texture preservation. Provider output alone is not the finished v6 portrait: the common skin finish is applied before local cosmetics. Report these stages separately when reviewing results.
