# Korean Makeup refinement on the existing shared-base workflow

Keep the deployed shared Natural AI base and local finish switching. This does not introduce independent AI jobs for Natural and Makeup, change processing order or change payments/downloads.

Current original-AI Makeup uses `shared-pink-nude-gloss-v7` (Soft Rosy uses `shared-peach-nude-gloss-v7`). The reference controls the nude-rose colour and natural blending only, never facial identity. It uses blended pink-brown upper lids with a fine brown liner, a more visible softly feathered nude-rose cheek tint, centre-to-edge gradient nude-rose lips, visible hydrated gloss, a continuous skin-weighted RGB lighting finish and soft brown Korean lash clusters following existing lid landmarks. Earlier v1/v2 presets retain their pixel behaviour. Skin texture samples, eye interiors, face proportions, native dimensions and alpha are retained. Natural restores the original shared base exactly. Older local presets and historical staged palettes keep their previous pixel behaviour.

Both female choices request the original face geometry through the existing original-upload AI prompts. A bounded local correction first restores the measured source face aspect; local makeup then changes colour only. See [source aspect and long-hair repair](portrait-aspect-hair-repair.md). Female delivery no longer rejects a completed image based on source/output landmark ratio comparisons; these estimates are unreliable across pose, crop and cosmetics. Male proportion checks are unchanged.

Browser pixel tests at 384px and 900px verify increased skin brightness, lip gloss and lash definition, increased lip/eyelid/cheek colour compared with v3 on light and dark skin fixtures, exact native geometry/alpha and exact Natural restoration. Existing shared-base, cache, source-eye, anatomy and clear-gathered checks remain. No paid model generation is performed.

Female delivery still requires a readable face, valid output, garment cleanup, selected/gathered-hair completion, current ownership/rights and an active uncancelled request. Neither estimated eye/gaze differences nor facial ratio differences can turn an otherwise completed female job into a processing failure. This does not assert that AI preserves every original feature exactly.

V5 feathers skin-label transitions and attenuates the hairline transition, replaces the stronger uniform brightening with a lower lift plus a bounded, twice-smoothed skin-weighted illumination correction, and retains native high-frequency texture. This reduces mask seams and broad forehead highlight imbalance without flattening all anatomical light. Korean upper lashes are longer/darker; muted rose-brown eyeshadow is clearer; the liner is placed on skin immediately above the lid so the eye-interior protection mask no longer erases it. Eye interiors, iris colour, geometry and alpha stay unchanged. V1–V4 palettes retain their previous rendering behavior. No additional provider jobs are introduced. Existing shared-base work can apply the new finish by switching Natural then Makeup.

V6 replaces the upper-lid radial spot with one tapered, softly feathered band following the complete measured lid curve. This spreads the nude-rose pigment toward both corners while retaining v5 lighting, liner, lashes, protected eye interiors and original dimensions/alpha.

V7 replaces the v6 pigment band with an eye-local rose field following the real upper lid and fading toward the brow-bounded socket. The central mobile lid carries substantially less pigment and a separate soft pearl-pink reflected-light field; inner/outer lid pigment no longer concentrates into a central spot. Both tilted eyes use their own lid and brow geometry. Feature-adjacent lighting transitions are wider, cheek/lip pigment and brown liner/lashes are lighter, and lip sheen is restrained for a fresh natural finish. No blur, face warp, source-eye repaint, new hair pass or additional AI job is introduced. Historical V1–V6 palettes retain their rendering behavior.

Validation includes three pure geometry/gradient checks (upward fade, pearl centre, tilt/reflection), nine real Chromium pixel checks at 384/900px on light/dark fixtures, shared-base/cache/transaction checks and production build. The v7 fixture centres gain 4–8 RGB luminance levels versus v6 while their rose chroma is lower than both side lids. Natural restoration, alpha and eye interiors remain exact. A retained native 2020 × 2288 portrait replay checks hair/eye-interior/neck pixels separately. For older saved Makeup pixels, select Natural then Makeup to render the new palette from the same saved base.
