# Portraits from the original upload

New female requests use `studioSkinVersion: studio-original-ai-v1`. Existing upload format/size/orientation preparation remains: unsupported formats may be converted, EXIF orientation baked and very large images bounded for Studio. These file-preparation steps do not retouch skin or lighting. The original source below is the retained upload from that preparation. They send the exact original upload as both `image` and `originalSource`. The client does not level or re-encode this input, capture skin masks, retouch blemishes, blur skin, add grain, blend feature-adjacent patches or generate a face crop before submission. The owned work's immutable raw-source hash is still checked before queueing. For an older work with a separately encoded canonical image, the proven raw original is used without rewriting that canonical image or changing rights.

One existing portrait AI request receives the original plus an optional selected hair reference. It performs lighting, cast correction, skin/acne/wrinkle repair, photographic detail and the selected Natural or rosy makeup finish together. The old skin/light swatch guide and outfit lighting references are omitted for this route. No subsequent skin smoothing, matte field, colour blend, artificial grain or local makeup renderer is applied. Existing background matting, edge cleanup, output alignment, clothing-alpha cleanup and separate outfit composition remain.

Natural and Makeup are distinct AI variants. A switch can generate a new variant from the same original or reuse an already accepted variant for the same original, account, hair and finish. Old v5 results remain visible but cannot satisfy the new cache or same-selection guards. New results preserve the original-AI completion version through saved work and do not acquire a shared local skin base. Explicit earlier local skin recipes are not automatically reapplied to a new AI result.

Released v1-v5 preparation, prompts and old-job completion remain available for pending requests. Payment, wallet, source ownership, cancellation and job confirmation semantics are unchanged. Verification includes byte-exact original requests, ownership failures and older canonical inputs, no local post-cosmetics calls, source/account cache isolation, actual App source replacement and finish switching. Provider visual validation is recorded separately from inert tests.

Natural revision `natural-studio-skin-v2-source-eyes` explicitly preserves the source eye opening, gaze, iris/pupil geometry and original catchlights while repairing acne, acne-related marks and fine skin wrinkles. Eyelid boundaries and natural eyelid folds remain protected facial features. Existing eye/iris/gaze landmark checks now also apply to Natural before accepting the result; they do not compare eye colour or catchlight pixels. Makeup instructions and the single original-upload generation pipeline stay unchanged. Both result caches and saved-work selection guards use the Natural revision, so old work remains visible but cannot satisfy a new Natural request. Tests cover the production request, acceptance rejection, unchanged makeup fingerprints and stale/current selection without calling a paid provider; visual fidelity still needs actual model evaluation.
