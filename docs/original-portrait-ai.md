# Portraits from the original upload

New female requests use `studioSkinVersion: studio-original-ai-v3`. Existing upload format/size/orientation preparation remains: unsupported formats may be converted, EXIF orientation baked and very large images bounded for Studio. These file-preparation steps do not retouch skin or lighting. The original source below is the retained upload from that preparation. Requests send the exact original upload as both `image` and `originalSource`. The client does not level or re-encode this input, capture skin masks, retouch blemishes, blur skin, add grain, blend feature-adjacent patches or generate a face crop before submission. The owned work's immutable raw-source hash is still checked before queueing. For an older work with a separately encoded canonical image, the proven raw original is used without rewriting that canonical image or changing rights.

The first portrait AI request receives the original plus an optional selected hair reference and the fixed identity-free skin/light guide derived from the user's target portrait. This first request is exactly the released v2 guided prompt/input policy for every style, including the Natural source-eye instructions. The guide is the final conditioning image and supplies material softness, fine texture and broad diffused light only. Its complexion swatches are not RGB or whitening targets, and it contains no identity. The first AI generation performs lighting, cast correction, skin/acne/wrinkle repair, photographic detail and selected cosmetics together. Outfit lighting references remain omitted.

Only new v3 Makeup requests (`makeup`, `korean-rosy-v1`, `soft-rosy-v1`) then receive a dedicated second AI skin-polish pass, before background matting. Its primary image is the first generated PNG and locks the accepted pose, face/hair geometry, head scale, expression, neck and cutout. A second conditioning image is the original upload converted only to an EXIF-oriented PNG bounded within 1024 × 1024, supplying identity rather than old lighting or defects. The fixed identity-free guide is the third image. No hair, outfit or additional makeup image is supplied in this pass.

The polish explicitly repairs residual forehead, glabellar, under-eye, crow's-feet and mouth-adjacent skin furrows, roughness and temporary acne-related red/brown marks while protecting true eyelid borders/folds, brows, nostrils, lips and stable identifying marks. It requests soft foundation-like even skin coverage and a controlled approximately 8–12% lift of deficient diffuse midtones, with balanced fill and restrained oily highlights. The selected rosy palette and existing eye/lip shapes remain; this is not another cosmetics layer. Photographic volume and believable fine skin texture remain, without local pixel retouch, fake grain, white fog or plastic skin.

Natural remains exactly one guided AI call with the released v2 prompt/input and source-eye policy. Released v1/v2 Makeup also remains single-call. V3 Makeup's two provider calls belong to the same customer job and existing reservation/confirmation transaction; no additional wallet operation is introduced. The `skin-finish` stage occurs between the two generation/matting stages. A failed or empty second result fails the whole job without matting, publishing or delivering the first result. Background matting, edge cleanup, output alignment, clothing-alpha cleanup and separate outfit composition operate on the accepted final result only. No subsequent skin smoothing, matte field, colour blend, artificial grain or local makeup renderer is applied.

V2 removes source-light retention wording from the neck reconstruction and the hair/pose scope guards without changing their geometry or cutout limits. A final skin/light authority is appended after all hair and default-scaffolding instructions. The source constrains identity, intrinsic facial anatomy and natural pigmentation; the target controls photographic lighting and skin-material finish. Unwanted source underexposure, colour casts, harsh directional shadows, oily shine and temporary skin defects may be corrected. Face, ears and reconstructed neck use one broad frontal diffused key and balanced fill, preserving subtle facial volume, authentic complexion depth and fine realistic texture.

Natural and Makeup are distinct AI variants. A switch can generate a new variant from the same original or reuse an already accepted current variant for the same original, account, hair and finish. Old staged or original-AI v1/v2 results remain visible but cannot satisfy v3 cache or same-selection guards. Results preserve their actual v1/v2/v3 completion version through saved work and do not acquire a shared local skin base. Explicit earlier local skin recipes are not automatically reapplied to an AI result from any supported original-AI version.

Released staged v1-v5 preparation and prompts remain frozen. Previous `studio-original-ai-v1` requests keep their exact no-guide prompt/payload, and `studio-original-ai-v2` keeps its exact guided single-call prompt/payload. Source binding accepts all three original-AI versions. Completed old jobs retain their actual version rather than being relabelled v3, and every original-AI version bypasses local post-cosmetics. Payment, wallet, source ownership, cancellation and job confirmation semantics are unchanged. Verification covers exact v1/v2 prompt and full request fingerprints, unchanged v3 Natural, makeup-only second-call provenance and image order, bounded original identity conversion, matting only after final success, atomic failure with no first-result fallback, byte-exact originals, source/account cache isolation and real App finish switching. Provider visual validation on ZIP test photos remains separate from inert tests; passing code checks alone does not establish the requested visual finish.

Natural revision `natural-studio-skin-v2-source-eyes` explicitly preserves the source eye opening, gaze, iris/pupil geometry and original catchlights while repairing acne, acne-related marks and fine skin wrinkles. Eyelid boundaries and natural eyelid folds remain protected facial features. Existing eye/iris/gaze landmark checks now also apply to Natural before accepting the result; they do not compare eye colour or catchlight pixels. This Natural policy and its single guided call stay unchanged. Only the v3 Makeup skin-polish stage described above adds another AI call; released v1/v2 Makeup remains unchanged. Both result caches and saved-work selection guards use the Natural revision, so old work remains visible but cannot satisfy a new Natural request. Tests cover the production request, acceptance rejection, unchanged makeup fingerprints and stale/current selection without calling a paid provider; visual fidelity still needs actual model evaluation.
