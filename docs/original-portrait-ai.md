# Portraits from the original upload

New female requests use `studioSkinVersion: studio-original-ai-v2`. Existing upload format/size/orientation preparation remains: unsupported formats may be converted, EXIF orientation baked and very large images bounded for Studio. These file-preparation steps do not retouch skin or lighting. The original source below is the retained upload from that preparation. Requests send the exact original upload as both `image` and `originalSource`. The client does not level or re-encode this input, capture skin masks, retouch blemishes, blur skin, add grain, blend feature-adjacent patches or generate a face crop before submission. The owned work's immutable raw-source hash is still checked before queueing. For an older work with a separately encoded canonical image, the proven raw original is used without rewriting that canonical image or changing rights.

One existing portrait AI request receives the original plus an optional selected hair reference and, for v2, the fixed identity-free skin/light guide derived from the user's target portrait. The guide is the final conditioning image and supplies material softness, fine texture and broad diffused light only. Its complexion swatches are not RGB or whitening targets, and it contains no identity. A v2-specific instruction permits the selected Natural or rosy makeup finish in this same generation; it does not inherit the older staged shared-bare-base rule. The AI performs lighting, cast correction, skin/acne/wrinkle repair, photographic detail and selected cosmetics together. Outfit lighting references remain omitted. No subsequent skin smoothing, matte field, colour blend, artificial grain or local makeup renderer is applied. Existing background matting, edge cleanup, output alignment, clothing-alpha cleanup and separate outfit composition remain.

V2 removes source-light retention wording from the neck reconstruction and the hair/pose scope guards without changing their geometry or cutout limits. A final skin/light authority is appended after all hair and default-scaffolding instructions. The source constrains identity, intrinsic facial anatomy and natural pigmentation; the target controls photographic lighting and skin-material finish. Unwanted source underexposure, colour casts, harsh directional shadows, oily shine and temporary skin defects may be corrected. Face, ears and reconstructed neck use one broad frontal diffused key and balanced fill, preserving subtle facial volume, authentic complexion depth and fine realistic texture.

Natural and Makeup are distinct AI variants. A switch can generate a new variant from the same original or reuse an already accepted current variant for the same original, account, hair and finish. Old staged or original-AI v1 results remain visible but cannot satisfy v2 cache or same-selection guards. Results preserve their actual v1/v2 completion version through saved work and do not acquire a shared local skin base. Explicit earlier local skin recipes are not automatically reapplied to an AI result from either version.

Released staged v1-v5 preparation and prompts remain frozen. Previous `studio-original-ai-v1` requests keep their exact prompt and no-guide payload; source binding accepts both original-AI versions. Completed old jobs retain their actual version rather than being relabelled v2, and both original-AI versions bypass local post-cosmetics. Payment, wallet, source ownership, cancellation and job confirmation semantics are unchanged. Verification includes exact v1 prompt and complete request fingerprints, v2 guide bytes/order and final target authority, byte-exact original requests, ownership failures and older canonical inputs, no local post-cosmetics calls, source/account cache isolation, actual App source replacement and finish switching. Provider visual validation on the user's ZIP test photos is recorded separately from inert tests; deployment hashes and fixture tests alone do not establish a match to the requested visual style.

Natural revision `natural-studio-skin-v2-source-eyes` explicitly preserves the source eye opening, gaze, iris/pupil geometry and original catchlights while repairing acne, acne-related marks and fine skin wrinkles. Eyelid boundaries and natural eyelid folds remain protected facial features. Existing eye/iris/gaze landmark checks now also apply to Natural before accepting the result; they do not compare eye colour or catchlight pixels. Makeup instructions and the single original-upload generation pipeline stay unchanged. Both result caches and saved-work selection guards use the Natural revision, so old work remains visible but cannot satisfy a new Natural request. Tests cover the production request, acceptance rejection, unchanged makeup fingerprints and stale/current selection without calling a paid provider; visual fidelity still needs actual model evaluation.
