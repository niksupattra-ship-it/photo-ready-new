# Portraits from the original upload

New female requests use `studioSkinVersion: studio-original-ai-v4`. Existing upload format/size/orientation preparation remains: unsupported formats may be converted, EXIF orientation baked and very large images bounded for Studio. These file-preparation steps do not retouch skin or lighting. The original source below is the retained upload from that preparation. Requests send the exact original upload as both `image` and `originalSource`. The client does not level or re-encode this input, capture skin masks, retouch blemishes, blur skin, add grain, blend feature-adjacent patches or generate a face crop before submission. The owned work's immutable raw-source hash is still checked before queueing. For an older work with a separately encoded canonical image, the proven raw original is used without rewriting that canonical image or changing rights.

New v4 Makeup requests (`makeup`, `korean-rosy-v1`, `soft-rosy-v1`) first send the exact full clothed original photograph and fixed identity-free skin/light guide to a dedicated AI skin-preparation request. No selected-hair, outfit, generated portrait or separate identity image is supplied in this phase. Its preservation-only instructions keep the person's face/eye/lip geometry and pigment, expression, pose, head scale, hairstyle, every garment and its coverage, background and framing. It requests no cosmetics yet, no clothing removal, no new exposed skin and no cutout.

The preparation explicitly repairs residual forehead, glabellar, under-eye, crow's-feet and mouth-adjacent skin furrows, roughness and temporary acne-related red/brown marks while protecting real eyelid borders/folds, brows, nostrils, lips and stable identifying marks. It requests soft foundation-like even skin coverage and a controlled approximately 8–12% lift of deficient diffuse midtones, balanced fill and restrained oily highlights, with believable fine texture rather than artificial grain, global fog or plastic masking. These are AI edits to the intact clothed photograph, with no local skin blend or filter.

The second request performs the existing portrait, selected-hair and rosy makeup task on that AI-prepared clothed PNG. Its prompt truthfully identifies Image 1 as AI-prepared, preserving its corrected smooth skin and diffuse light. The original upload is independently converted only to an EXIF-oriented PNG bounded within 1024 × 1024 for identity conditioning; its anatomy and expression are authoritative, its old wrinkles/roughness/casts/shadows are not. Identity indices are established before the guide index: prepared portrait first, optional selected hair second, original identity next, guide last. The final priority retains the first AI's repaired skin and light, continues it into reconstructed ear/neck areas and adds the existing rosy cosmetics once. It must not reintroduce old source skin defects, layer cosmetics or repeat the preparation's midtone lift. No post-cutout AI edit is made for v4.

Natural v4 remains exactly one guided call with the released v2 prompt/input and source-eye policy. Released v1/v2 Makeup remains single-call. V4 Makeup's preparation and final portrait calls belong to the same customer job and existing reservation/confirmation transaction; no additional wallet operation is introduced. The `skin-prepare` stage precedes `generation`, then matting/edges occur only after final success. Any preparation or final-generation failure or provider refusal fails the whole job with no intermediate result delivery, no automatic retry and no fallback to an earlier generated image. Background matting, edge cleanup, output alignment, clothing-alpha cleanup and separate outfit composition operate on the accepted final portrait only. No subsequent skin smoothing, matte field, colour blend, artificial grain or local makeup renderer is applied.

The former v3 post-generation polish route is withdrawn for NEW HTTP submissions: old open clients receive the existing refresh-version error before credit reservation. Its process-level behavior remains frozen only for already queued v3 work and versioned metadata. The provider-refused generated cutout is not retried, disguised, masked or reused as input to the new workflow. The new preparation uses the intact clothed original and stops on any refusal.

V2 removes source-light retention wording from the neck reconstruction and the hair/pose scope guards without changing their geometry or cutout limits. A final skin/light authority is appended after all hair and default-scaffolding instructions. The source constrains identity, intrinsic facial anatomy and natural pigmentation; the target controls photographic lighting and skin-material finish. Unwanted source underexposure, colour casts, harsh directional shadows, oily shine and temporary skin defects may be corrected. Face, ears and reconstructed neck use one broad frontal diffused key and balanced fill, preserving subtle facial volume, authentic complexion depth and fine realistic texture.

Natural and Makeup are distinct AI variants. A switch can generate a new variant from the same original or reuse an already accepted current variant for the same original, account, hair and finish. Old staged or original-AI v1/v2/v3 results remain visible but cannot satisfy v4 cache or same-selection guards. Results preserve their actual v1/v2/v3/v4 completion version through saved work and do not acquire a shared local skin base. Explicit earlier local skin recipes are not automatically reapplied to an AI result from any supported original-AI version.

Released staged v1-v5 preparation and prompts remain frozen. Previous `studio-original-ai-v1` requests keep their exact no-guide prompt/payload, `studio-original-ai-v2` keeps its exact guided single-call prompt/payload, and queued `studio-original-ai-v3` retains its frozen process-level behavior. Source binding and completion metadata recognize these historical versions without treating them as current v4 cache results. New v3 HTTP submissions are rejected before reservation as described above. No original-AI version applies local post-cosmetics. Wallet, ownership, cancellation and job-confirmation semantics are unchanged. Verification covers exact v1/v2 and queued-v3 request fingerprints, unchanged v4 Natural, original-first clothed preparation, prepared-image final provenance, correctly indexed original identity and guide, matting only after final success and atomic failure of either phase with no retries or intermediate delivery. Provider visual validation on the user's ZIP tests remains separate from inert checks; passing code checks alone does not establish the requested visual finish.

This Natural policy and its single guided call stay unchanged.
